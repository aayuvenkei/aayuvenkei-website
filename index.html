AV

I don't decorate interfaces. I make them respond.

A website is not a poster. It is a machine that reacts to a person. Every hover, scroll and keystroke is a chance to prove something is alive behind the glass.

I write it by hand. No framework in the way, no build step deciding for me. Just HTML, CSS and JavaScript — small enough to understand completely, fast enough to feel instant.

Then I automate the boring parts. Bots, scrapers and pipelines that keep running after the tab is closed. That is where the real leverage lives.
